The National Skills Registration Program (NSRP) is a program initiated by the Department of Labor and Employment (DOLE) that aims to create and maintain a nationwide database of skilled workers, jobseekers, and employers through the Skills Registry System (SRS) and the PESO Employment Information System (PEIS).

It serves as an employment facilitation program that helps connect qualified individuals with job opportunities based on their skills, qualifications, and work experience.

The NSRP supports Public Employment Service Offices (PESOs) in matching jobseekers with employers, while also helping the government identify workforce needs and develop appropriate employment and training programs nationwide.

The Yearbook and Graduation Photo Participation, Consent, and Waiver Form is a document provided by the university for students to confirm their participation in the yearbook publication and graduation photo session for the academic year 2025–2026.

It collects essential student information and allows individuals to select their preferred participation option, whether availing the full package, photo session only, submitting their own photo, or waiving participation entirely, subject to university guidelines and approval.

The form also secures the student’s consent for the use of their photo and personal details in official publications, while outlining data privacy practices, terms of use, and a waiver releasing the university from liability related to publication and participation.

Status: Submitted

The Data Privacy Consent Form for Students is a document issued by the university to secure a student’s permission to collect, process, store, and safeguard their personal data throughout their academic stay, in compliance with the Data Privacy Act of 2012.

It outlines the types of personal information that may be collected, including basic identification details, academic records, and other relevant data necessary for academic, administrative, and research purposes, as well as the possible use of photos and videos captured within the university.

The form also informs students of their rights regarding their personal data, including access, correction, deletion, and withdrawal of consent, while ensuring that appropriate security measures are in place to protect their information from unauthorized use or disclosure.
