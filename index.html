The National Skills Registration Program (NSRP) is a program initiated by the Department of Labor and Employment (DOLE) that aims to create and maintain a nationwide database of skilled workers, jobseekers, and employers through the Skills Registry System (SRS) and the PESO Employment Information System (PEIS).

It serves as an employment facilitation program that helps connect qualified individuals with job opportunities based on their skills, qualifications, and work experience.

The NSRP supports Public Employment Service Offices (PESOs) in matching jobseekers with employers, while also helping the government identify workforce needs and develop appropriate employment and training programs nationwide.
